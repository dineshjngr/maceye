# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want MacEye to be a friendly project for everyone. We pledge to make taking part a harassment-free experience for all people, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Being kind and patient with others.
- Respecting different opinions and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Owning mistakes, apologising, and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the whole community.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Insults, personal attacks, trolling, or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as a home or email address, without their clear permission.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews. It also applies when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for explaining and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and they may ban people temporarily or permanently for behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.

## Reporting

If you see or experience unacceptable behaviour:

- Use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment, issue or pull request. This goes to the maintainers and to GitHub.
- For anything you want to raise with the maintainers privately, use **Report a vulnerability** on the repository's [Security tab](https://github.com/dineshjngr/maceye/security) and start the title with "Code of Conduct". Only maintainers can read these reports.

All reports are reviewed and handled as quickly and fairly as possible. Maintainers will respect the privacy and safety of the person reporting.

## Enforcement guidelines

1. **Correction.** A private note explaining what was inappropriate and why. A public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning.** A warning with consequences for continued behaviour, and no interaction with the people involved for a set time.
3. **Temporary ban.** A ban from any interaction with the project for a set time.
4. **Permanent ban.** A permanent ban from the project for a pattern of violations or for serious harassment.
